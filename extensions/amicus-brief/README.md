# Amicus Brief Generator

The amicus brief generator is designed to create educational, dual-perspective analysis for healthcare ethics and medical law questions.

## Included Features

- Patient/plaintiff pathway
- Institution/defense pathway
- Beneficence analysis
- Autonomy analysis
- Non-maleficence analysis
- Justice analysis
- Jurisdictional legal considerations
- Educational labeling and disclaimer

## Educational Disclaimer

All generated outputs are educational and informational only. They do not constitute legal advice, medical advice, or formal legal opinions.

## Example Use

Use the generator to review:
- patient rights
- provider obligations
- consent and capacity
- harm minimization
- fair allocation of care
- jurisdiction-specific legal issues

## Output Structure

Each generated brief should clearly label:
- the case title
- jurisdiction
- factual summary
- legal pathway analysis
- ethical principle analysis
- final educational disclaimer

The output should emphasize that it is informative, not binding legal advice.
